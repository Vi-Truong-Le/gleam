PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy — Gleam

Effective date: September 13, 2026

The short version: everything you enter into Gleam stays on your device. We do not operate a server for this app, we do not collect your data, and we do not share it with anyone — because it never leaves your phone.

Gleam ("Gleam," "we," "us") is a personal medication-tracking companion for people using GLP-1 medications (oral pills or weekly injections). This Privacy Policy explains what happens to your information when you use the Gleam iOS app.

1. Information you enter into the app

Gleam is a self-tracking tool. You may choose to enter:

  • Medications, doses, and schedules (oral pill or weekly injection)
  • Dose log history
  • Side effects you record
  • Weight, height, unit preference, and goal weight
  • Reminder preferences

All of this is stored only in local files on your device (inside the app's own private storage area), never on a server operated by us or anyone else, because Gleam has no backend and makes no network connections. Gleam also computes an estimated medication-concentration chart entirely on your device, from published pharmacokinetic reference values — this calculation runs locally and its results are never transmitted anywhere.

2. Data we do not collect

Gleam does not collect, and has no mechanism to collect:

  • Your name, email address, or any account credentials — Gleam does not require or support an account or login
  • Advertising identifiers or analytics identifiers
  • Location, camera, photo library, or contacts data
  • Health app (HealthKit) records
  • Any usage analytics, crash reports, or behavioral tracking

Gleam does not integrate any advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK, and it makes no network requests of any kind. This was verified directly against the app's source code, not assumed.

3. Notifications

Gleam asks for permission to send local notifications — dose reminders and a "you can eat now" alert when a fasting window (for oral medications) ends. These notifications are scheduled entirely on your device using Apple's local notification framework. There is no push-notification server, and no notification content is sent to us or to Apple's push service.

4. Sharing of information

Because Gleam collects no data on any server, there is nothing for us to share, sell, or disclose to advertisers, data brokers, or any other third party. We do not have access to what you enter into the app.

5. Your choices and control over your data

  • Reset all data: In Settings, you can permanently erase all medications, dose logs, side effects, and weigh-ins stored on your device at any time.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored Gleam data along with it, since nothing is stored anywhere else.
  • Since we never receive your data, there is no server-side copy for us to delete, export, or provide to you on request — what is on your device is the only copy that exists.

6. Health information

Some information you may log in Gleam (medications, doses, side effects, weight) is health-related. This data is never transmitted off your device, is not used for advertising, and is not shared with any third party. Gleam is a personal tracking tool and is not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment — see the in-app "Medical disclaimer & sources" section and our Terms of Use for details.

7. Children's privacy

Gleam is intended for adults managing their own GLP-1 medication regimen and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children, and in any case Gleam has no mechanism to transmit any user's information anywhere, to us or otherwise.

8. Data security

Your data is protected by iOS's standard app-sandboxing and, if enabled, your device's passcode/ Face ID/Touch ID. We recommend keeping a passcode enabled on your device, since anyone with access to an unlocked device could view locally stored app data — the same as for any other app on your phone.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of Gleam adds a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, an optional iCloud sync or Apple Health integration), we will update this Privacy Policy before that feature ships, and update the "Effective date" above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
