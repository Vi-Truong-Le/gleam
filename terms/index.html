1. What Gleam is

Gleam is a personal logbook for medications you have been prescribed (oral pills or weekly injections): dose logs, schedules and reminders, a supply countdown you set up yourself, a map of the injection sites you logged, side-effect notes, and weight/BMI progress. Gleam has no account system — everything you enter is stored locally on your device only, as described in our Privacy Policy.

Medical disclaimer: Gleam is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always follow your prescriber's instructions.
