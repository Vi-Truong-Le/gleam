FAQ
What happens to my data when I switch medications?

Nothing is lost. The Med-Switch wizard carries your dose history and streak into your new medication, and the concentration chart shows the honest overlap between your old and new medication.

Do I need an account?

No. Gleam has no login and no account system — open the app and start logging.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Gleam makes no network connections. Everything you log stays only on your device, and dose reminders are local notifications that never leave your phone.

Is the concentration chart a medical measurement?

No. It's an estimate computed on-device from your logged doses and publicly published half-life/Tmax data — not a lab result, and not a basis for adjusting your own dose or the timing of a switch.

Which medications does Gleam support?

Both daily oral GLP-1 pills (e.g. Rybelsus / oral semaglutide) and weekly injections (e.g. Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound) — and switching between them.
