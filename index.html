FAQ
What happens to my data when I switch medications?

Nothing is lost. The Med-Switch wizard carries your dose history, streak and weigh-ins into your new plan — one continuous record instead of a blank restart.

Do I need an account?

No. Gleam has no login and no account system — open the app and start logging.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Gleam makes no network connections. Everything you log stays only on your device, and dose reminders are local notifications that never leave your phone.

Does Gleam tell me how much to take?

No — and it never will. Gleam has no medication database and does no medication arithmetic. You type in the name and amount your prescriber gave you, and Gleam simply keeps the record.

Which medications does Gleam work with?

Any of them. You type your own medication name and dose as written on your prescription, and pick daily or weekly — so a pill, a shot, or a change between them all fit.
