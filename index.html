FAQ
Do I need an account?

No. Gleam has no login and no account system — open the app and start logging.

Is my health data private?

Yes. Gleam makes no network connections. Everything you log stays only on your device, and dose reminders are local notifications that never leave your phone.

Is the concentration chart a medical measurement?

No. It's an estimate computed on-device from your logged doses and publicly published half-life/Tmax data — not a lab result, and not a basis for adjusting your own dose.

Does the free version include the concentration chart?

Yes — unlike trackers that lock it behind a paywall, Gleam's estimated-level chart is part of the free core experience.

Which medications does Gleam support?

Both daily oral GLP-1 pills (e.g. Rybelsus / oral semaglutide) and weekly injections (e.g. Ozempic, Wegovy, Mounjaro, Zepbound).
